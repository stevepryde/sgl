Capture method

Images are unretouched PNG readbacks from SGL3D’s WebGPU renderer. Comparison pairs use the same fixture, camera, resolution, exposure, and warm-up frame count; only the named setting changes. Thumbnails are resized WebP previews. No AI-generated imagery or post-capture colour adjustments are used.

The showcase distributes renders, not meshes, textures, environments, or bake payloads. Scene rights are separate from SGL’s source-code licence.
